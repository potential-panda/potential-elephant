## Summary

Major macroeconomic and technological trends are modelled as "rivers" — directed flows of capital from source through upstream enablers, middle-stream bottlenecks, to lower-stream capacity constraints.

### Four layers

| Layer | Description | Alpha |
|---|---|---|
| source | Corporate CapEx or regulatory funding (hyperscalers, policy mandates) | Low |
| upper | Core designers absorbing the initial capital surge | Medium |
| middle | Highly inelastic specialized suppliers where bottlenecks emerge | **Highest — 2-3x** |
| lower | Ultimate physical boundary checks (power grids, logistics) | Medium |

### Individual edge over institutions
- Zero market impact → can enter mid-caps instantly
- No sector silos → cross-allocate freely
- Coverage gaps → under-followed JP and US mid-caps

### Four established rivers
1. AI Infrastructure Supercycle
2. Tech Localization & Onshoring
3. Embodied Physical AI
4. Demographic Longevity

### Key execution rules
- Time-lag window: upstream signals take 2-6 weeks to manifest downstream
- Entry filter: clean technical breakout + volume ratio > 1.5x 100-day baseline
- Exit signal: retail BBS hyper-bullish consensus + weekly close below 8-week EMA
